# UNIFAID Universal Charter for Ethical AI and Open Knowledge

**In service of a peaceful, loving, connected and abundant humanity and Planet Earth.**

## Preamble

We believe that knowledge and intelligence should serve life. As artificial intelligence becomes part of education, culture, science, public services, creativity and everyday work, the systems through which people and machines communicate should support human dignity, peace, understanding, ecological responsibility and shared flourishing.

UNIFAID is an open technical and civic initiative. It seeks to make communication between AI systems interoperable while keeping people—not platforms or automated systems—at the centre of decisions that affect them.

This Charter is an ethical commitment for project participants. It is not a religious creed, a political platform, a substitute for law, or a claim to represent any faith or institution.

## Our commitments

### 1. Unity of humanity
We recognize the equal dignity of every person. The protocol must be designed for global participation and must not encode discrimination based on nationality, ethnicity, sex, disability, religion, belief, language, economic status or other protected or vulnerable characteristics.

### 2. Unity in diversity
Plurality of cultures, languages, beliefs, scientific perspectives and AI approaches is a strength. UNIFAID must enable cooperation without demanding uniformity of thought or identity.

### 3. Independent investigation of truth
Participants should be able to inspect evidence, compare perspectives, challenge outputs and distinguish verified information from opinion, uncertainty, speculation and generated content.

### 4. Harmony of science, ethics and wisdom
Technical capability alone is not sufficient. Design decisions should consider human consequences, scientific evidence, ethical reasoning and long-term effects on society and the natural world.

### 5. Human agency and informed consent
People must be able to understand which AI systems receive their messages, what data is shared, what tools are invoked and what retention settings apply. Consent must be specific, revocable where technically possible, and not hidden in deceptive interfaces.

### 6. Privacy and data sovereignty
Collect only what is necessary. Minimize disclosure. Support export, deletion requests, local and self-hosted deployments, and clear data boundaries. Never imply that a protocol can force a third-party provider to delete data outside its control.

### 7. Peaceful and responsible use
UNIFAID should support constructive communication, conflict de-escalation, education, accessibility and cooperation. It must not be designed to facilitate violence, persecution, mass surveillance, manipulation or targeted abuse.

### 8. Ecological responsibility
Consider energy, hardware, water, minerals, supply chains and electronic waste across the lifecycle of AI systems. Prefer efficient designs and transparent reporting over unsupported claims of environmental benefit.

### 9. Open knowledge and shared stewardship
Publish specifications, governance decisions and conformance tests openly. Encourage education, public-interest research, open-source implementations and meaningful participation from communities affected by the technology.

### 10. Accountability and repair
Make responsibility traceable. Provide ways to report harms, correct errors, challenge decisions and improve systems. Automation must not become an excuse for avoiding human accountability.

## Practical design tests

Every major feature should answer:
- Does it increase meaningful human agency?
- Is data sharing understandable and proportionate?
- Can people participate across languages, abilities and levels of connectivity?
- Can independent implementers inspect and reproduce the behaviour?
- Are risks, limitations and conflicts of interest disclosed?
- Does it respect the planet's finite resources?

## Pluralism and inspiration

UNIFAID welcomes inspiration from the Bahá’í principle of the oneness of humanity and from many other ethical, spiritual, philosophical, Indigenous, scientific and humanist traditions. It does not claim religious authority, membership, representation or endorsement. No belief test is required to contribute or use the protocol.

## Commitment

We invite developers, researchers, educators, civil society, public institutions, AI providers and interested individuals to help build communication infrastructure that serves a peaceful, loving, connected and abundant humanity and Planet Earth.

**Technology should connect without controlling. Intelligence should serve life.**
