# UNIFAID Governance Proposal

## Purpose

Govern UNIFAID as an open, neutral, public-interest protocol—not as the product or property of one AI vendor, founder, religion or government.

## Proposed roles

- **Maintainers:** steward repositories, triage issues and review changes.
- **Specification editors:** maintain protocol documents and record decisions.
- **Working groups:** focus on protocol core, security/privacy, federation, adapters and ethics/accessibility.
- **Community participants:** propose changes, test implementations and raise concerns.
- **Independent reviewers:** provide security, legal, accessibility and interoperability review.

These roles are proposals. No foundation or formal governance body is claimed to exist at this stage.

## Decision process

1. Open an issue describing the problem, motivation and alternatives.
2. Discuss impact, compatibility, security and implementation costs.
3. For substantial changes, submit a UNIFAID Enhancement Proposal (UEP).
4. Seek review from affected working groups and implementers.
5. Record the decision, rationale, dissent and migration plan publicly.

Consensus is preferred; where consensus cannot be reached, maintainers should document the disagreement and use a transparent decision process. Security vulnerabilities may require coordinated private handling before public disclosure.

## Neutrality

- No single provider receives privileged protocol control.
- Provider adapters must follow documented APIs and terms.
- No religious or political affiliation is required.
- The project does not claim endorsement by any company, standards body or faith community.
- Conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

## Standards maturity

Drafts are not standards. A specification may be called stable only after:
- public review;
- at least two independent interoperable implementations for the relevant profile;
- published conformance tests;
- threat modelling and security review;
- documented versioning and change control.

## Trademark and name

The UNIFAID name is provisional pending trademark and domain review. Use of the name does not imply that the project is incorporated or that a legal entity exists.
