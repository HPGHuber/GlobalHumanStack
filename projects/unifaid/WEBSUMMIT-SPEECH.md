# UNIFAID — Web Summit Keynote / Founder Speech

**Working title:** One Humanity. Many Intelligences. One Open Future.  
**Target length:** approximately 5 minutes  
**Delivery:** warm, visionary, grounded; invite builders rather than claim a finished global standard.

---

Good morning, everyone.

We are living through one of the greatest transformations in human history.

Artificial intelligence is learning to write, reason, design, discover, translate and help us solve problems that once seemed impossible.

But there is a contradiction at the heart of this revolution.

Our intelligence is becoming more connected — while our AI systems are becoming more fragmented.

Our conversations are locked inside platforms. Our context is trapped in products. Our tools speak different languages. And every time we move from one AI system to another, we risk losing the continuity of our own thinking.

Imagine if email only worked when everyone used the same email company.

Imagine if the internet had been built as one private network, owned by one corporation.

We chose a different path.

We built open protocols. We built the web. We built shared infrastructure that allows different systems to communicate.

Now we need to do the same for artificial intelligence.

That is why we are building **UNIFAID — Universal AI Communication**.

UNIFAID is an open, provider-neutral communication layer designed to let people connect different AI systems in one coherent conversation.

Imagine asking one question and inviting different models to contribute.

OpenAI. Gemini. Claude. Mistral. Open-source models. Future systems we have not even imagined yet.

Not because they must become the same — but because they should be able to understand a shared language.

One conversation. Many intelligences. Human choice at the centre.

And this is not about building another walled garden.

It is about building the bridge between gardens.

The user decides which providers receive a message. Each response carries its provider and model attribution. Conversation history can be exported in an open format. Provider-specific capabilities remain visible instead of being hidden behind a false promise that every model is identical.

We are starting small and honestly.

The first demonstrator lets a person select providers, send a shared prompt, compare responses and export the conversation. It can run with simulated responses, or connect to official provider APIs when the user supplies their own authorized credentials.

It is an early prototype. It is not yet a ratified standard. It is not yet federated. And it has not been endorsed by the companies whose systems we hope to connect.

That is precisely why we are here.

We are inviting the builders of the next internet to help shape this together.

But technology alone is not enough.

We need a shared ethical direction.

The inspiration behind UNIFAID is a simple commitment:

**In service of a peaceful, loving, connected and abundant humanity and Planet Earth.**

This is not a religion, and it does not belong to one nation, company or belief system. It is an invitation to put human dignity, freedom, truth-seeking, pluralism and service at the centre of the infrastructure we create.

Think of the open-source spirit at its best: knowledge shared, contributions visible, power accountable, and progress made together.

We want UNIFAID to have three living pillars.

First: **UNIFAID Code** — open protocols, reference software and interoperability tests.

Second: **UNIFAID Ethics** — human-centred principles for consent, privacy, transparency, safety and accountability.

Third: **UNIFAID Community** — developers, researchers, artists, educators, civil society, businesses and people from every part of the world.

And we want to build this in the open.

We are looking for API and protocol engineers, security researchers, open-source maintainers, AI labs, universities, standards organisations and people who believe that interoperability is a public good.

Our invitation is practical: bring a use case, test the prototype, challenge the protocol, contribute an adapter, review the security model, and help us make the standard worthy of public trust.

Because the future of AI should not be decided only by whoever builds the biggest model.

It should also be shaped by the people who use it, the communities affected by it, and the shared values that guide it.

The internet connected computers.

UNIFAID aims to help connect intelligences — while keeping people in control.

One humanity. Many intelligences. Open communication. Shared responsibility.

Thank you.
