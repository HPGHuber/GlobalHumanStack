# NECO — Basic-income programme design

## Objective

Build a transparent programme engine that can administer grants or basic-income pilots without confusing a programme entitlement with bank money, e-money, a crypto-asset, or a guaranteed return.

## Separation of concerns

1. **Funding:** identify the lawful funding source, restrictions, liquidity and independently verifiable balances.
2. **Eligibility:** publish objective rules, evidence requirements, exclusions and an appeal process.
3. **Allocation:** calculate proposed amounts using a versioned ruleset.
4. **Approval:** require authorized human or governance approval; no autonomous AI approval of payments.
5. **Payment:** submit approved payment instructions through a regulated provider, subject to user consent and applicable controls.
6. **Reconciliation:** reconcile accepted, booked, rejected, returned and cancelled payments against provider records.
7. **Reporting:** publish privacy-preserving aggregate results and audited programme accounts.

## Minimum pilot

Start with a bounded, funded pilot for a defined community and period. Do not promise a universal amount until recurring funding, eligibility, tax treatment, legal structure and operational costs are evidenced.

## Required controls

- No distribution without confirmed available funds.
- No algorithmic eligibility decision without explainable reasons and human appeal.
- No double claim: use purpose-limited uniqueness checks with privacy safeguards.
- No public-chain personal data.
- No sale or speculative market promise of NECO units.
- Publish issuance, redemption, expiry, reversals and complaints rules before any transferable value exists.
- Independent financial audit and programme evaluation.

## Future tokenization

Tokenization is a later optional phase, not a prerequisite for the pilot. It requires a legal classification, identified issuer, reserve/funding design, redemption and consumer disclosures, custody model, AML/sanctions controls and independent security review. IOTA can provide optional integrity proofs; it does not by itself make a token legally redeemable or fully backed.
