# GLOBAL RECALIBRATION PROJECT
## ONCE × WORLD × NECO × ALLUNITY × VISA

**Status:** Partnership and pilot proposal — not an announcement of existing partnerships  
**Version:** 0.1 — 3 November 2026  
**Stewardship proposal:** Independent Global Commons Foundation

## 1. Purpose

Build an open, interoperable digital-public-interest ecosystem connecting:
- **ONCE:** open communication and messaging interoperability.
- **HeavenKey:** passkey-first account and access layer.
- **World / World ID:** optional privacy-preserving proof-of-human integration, subject to World’s participation and user choice.
- **NECO:** common-good payment, community funding and basic-income programme architecture.
- **AllUnity:** proposed regulated digital-money and settlement partner, beginning with EURAU integration.
- **Visa:** proposed card-network and payment-programme partner, subject to issuer, acquirer, programme-manager, geographic and network approvals.

The project is called **Global Recalibration**: recalibrating access to communication, identity, payments and common-good funding around openness, user agency, privacy and accountability.

## 2. Partnership thesis

AllUnity is a plausible regulated-money infrastructure partner. Its public materials describe EURAU as a fully reserved, euro-denominated MiCAR e-money token issued by a BaFin-licensed electronic money institution. AllUnity has announced EURAU deployments on Ethereum, Solana and BNB Smart Chain, and publishes reserve, audit and white-paper information in its Trust Center.

Visa is a plausible acceptance and card-network partner. Visa has publicly announced multiple stablecoin-linked card and settlement collaborations. Those precedents establish that such programme structures exist; they do not establish Visa approval, acceptance or a partnership for this project.

No organisation named in this proposal has committed to participate.

## 3. Proposed product architecture

### User experience
1. A user creates a HeavenKey passkey.
2. The user may optionally connect World ID or another compatible credential.
3. ONCE provides messaging, groups and service notifications across participating open networks.
4. A NECO wallet interface displays supported balances and payment routes.
5. For the initial regulated euro payment pilot, the interface connects to an authorised provider and EURAU under the provider's terms.
6. A Visa card programme may later enable eligible cardholders to spend supported balances at Visa-accepting merchants, through an approved issuer and programme manager.

### Separation of responsibilities
- ONCE does not custody or issue money.
- World ID is not mandatory for basic messaging or payments.
- NECO Commons funds are legally and operationally separate from payment-token reserves.
- AllUnity remains the issuer and controls its regulated issuance, redemption, compliance and reserve obligations for its own tokens.
- Visa network access requires formal programme approval and participating regulated issuers/acquirers.
- The Global Commons Foundation, if established, stewards open standards and public-benefit governance; it does not represent itself as a bank, e-money issuer or card network.

## 4. First pilot: EURAU-powered NECO payment experience

**Pilot geography:** Germany / European Economic Area, subject to legal and partner approval.  
**Initial currency:** EUR.  
**Settlement asset:** EURAU, only where AllUnity authorises the integration and the relevant legal and technical conditions are met.  
**Card:** discovery and feasibility workstream; not a launch dependency.

### MVP scope
- HeavenKey passkey sign-in.
- Optional World ID proof flow with a non-World alternative.
- ONCE account-to-account messaging.
- NECO wallet user interface connected to a partner-approved wallet or custody model.
- EURAU balance display and transfer flow in a sandbox first.
- Payment status, fees, redemption disclosures and transaction history.
- Merchant QR/payment-link demonstration only after acquiring and legal approval.
- No public token sale, no unbacked NECO stablecoin, no promise of universal free payments and no guaranteed basic income.

### Pilot acceptance criteria
- Independent security review of the application and key-management model.
- Clear identification of issuer, custodian, wallet provider and payment service provider.
- Users can understand fees, risks, redemption terms and complaint routes.
- No biometric or World ID requirement for ordinary access.
- Successful test transactions across at least two independently operated environments.
- Published privacy impact assessment and data-flow map.
- Reconciliation and incident-response procedures tested.
- No use of the Visa brand or card acceptance claims before written approval.

## 5. NECO monetary and common-good model

NECO is the ecosystem and programme layer, not a claim that a new global legal tender already exists.

Separate four functions:
1. **Payment asset:** initially integrate an existing regulated euro e-money token such as EURAU, if approved by AllUnity.
2. **Wallet and routing:** present balances and permitted payment rails without obscuring the actual issuer or provider.
3. **NECO Commons:** separately governed donations, grants and public-good funding, with independent accounting.
4. **Basic-income pilots:** funded programmes with defined sponsors, eligibility, budget, duration and independent evaluation.

Do not represent EURAU as NECO, do not imply AllUnity guarantees NECO, and do not mix charitable or basic-income funds with reserves backing an e-money token.

Any future NECO-issued instrument requires a separate legal, regulatory, reserve, redemption, governance and risk assessment before issuance.

## 6. Workstreams and 12-month roadmap

### Workstream A — Governance and legal
- Establish independent stewardship and contributor governance.
- Commission German/EU legal analysis covering MiCAR, ZAG, PSD2/PSD3 transition, AML/CFT, GDPR, consumer protection and card-programme requirements.
- Define roles and liability across issuer, wallet, custodian, distributor, programme manager and merchants.

### Workstream B — Technology
- Publish Global Commons Protocol interfaces and threat model.
- Build HeavenKey and optional World ID integration.
- Extend ONCE with payment-intent and receipt message types; never expose private keys or sensitive payment credentials in messages.
- Build NECO wallet adapter with EURAU sandbox integration if partner access is granted.
- Design provider-independent, multichain interfaces; do not bridge assets without audited contracts and approved liquidity/custody arrangements.

### Workstream C — AllUnity partnership
Request a technical and business discovery meeting on:
- EURAU integration and permitted distribution model.
- Supported chains, wallets, APIs and sandbox access.
- Redemption, custody, compliance and transaction-monitoring responsibilities.
- Merchant and business payment use cases.
- Commercial terms and pilot governance.

### Workstream D — Visa partnership
Request a discovery meeting on:
- Appropriate issuer and programme-manager route.
- Stablecoin-linked card programme feasibility.
- Tokenisation, wallet provisioning, authorisation and settlement requirements.
- Eligible launch markets and licensing constraints.
- Fees, risk controls, dispute handling and consumer protections.

### Workstream E — Community and public benefit
- Recruit a small, diverse pilot cohort and participating merchants.
- Test low-cost cross-border transfers and accessibility.
- Design a separately funded community grant pilot.
- Publish measured fees, completion rates, inclusion outcomes, incidents and user feedback.

## 9. Public principles

- Open standards and documented interfaces.
- Free basic communication access, supported by a sustainable non-advertising model.
- Privacy by design and data minimisation.
- Optional identity proofs and alternative access routes.
- Transparent fees and clear provider responsibility.
- Independent audits and public reporting.
- Separation of payment reserves from grants and social programmes.
- No promise of universal free money; basic-income programmes must be fully funded.
