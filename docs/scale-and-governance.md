# Global Scale and Governance Principles

AYA WORLD CONNECT is designed to grow from a narrowly scoped, safe service into an interoperable global platform. Scale is earned through evidence, not assumed.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Human agency and informed consent.
- No mandatory biometric or single-provider identity.
- No exclusion of people without smartphones, connectivity or formal identity documents.
- Data minimization and purpose limitation.
- Open standards and replaceable providers.
- Accessibility, localization and low-bandwidth operation.
- Clear separation between authentication, proof of personhood, legal identity, credentials, AI authorization and financial services.
- Independent governance, transparent change control and public security reporting.

## Scale sequence

1. Single-region sandbox engineering.
2. Small production pilot with opt-in users.
3. Independent audit and general availability.
4. Regional deployments with data residency and local legal review.
5. Federated operators and interoperable trust frameworks.
6. Multi-region global operations with tested resilience and transparent governance.

## Governance

Before onboarding the public at scale, establish:
- A legal operating entity and clear data-controller responsibilities.
- Independent security and privacy oversight.
- Public vulnerability disclosure and coordinated incident response.
- A transparent standards and change-management process.
- Community representation and accessible complaint/appeal channels.
- A sustainability plan for infrastructure, support and abuse prevention.

No partner, regulator, standards body or provider should be described as endorsing the project without a written agreement.
